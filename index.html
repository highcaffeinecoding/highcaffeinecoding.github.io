Nebula

A personal media centre for Kodi, built by High Caffeine Coding.

Nebula is a set of Kodi add-ons that run on a home TV. Its YouTube section lets the owner of the TV browse their subscriptions, history and recommendations using their own Google account.

Signing in with Google lets Nebula read your YouTube account through the YouTube Data API. Nebula has no servers: your sign-in stays on your own Kodi device.
