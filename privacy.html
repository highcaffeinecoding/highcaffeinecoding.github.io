Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

What Nebula accesses

When you sign in with Google, Nebula uses the YouTube Data API to read your YouTube account: subscriptions, playlists, watch history and recommendations. It may also update your account when you ask it to, for example by rating a video or adding it to a playlist.

Where your data goes

Nebula has no servers and collects nothing. Your Google sign-in tokens and YouTube data are stored only on your own Kodi device. Requests go directly from that device to Google. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to High Caffeine Coding.

Removing access

You can revoke Nebula's access at any time from your Google account's security settings, at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the add-ons deletes the data stored on your device.

YouTube and Google

Nebula uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub.
